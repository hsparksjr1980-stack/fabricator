# Fabricator Product Brief

Fabricator is a premium but practical builder operating system for people managing vehicle, fabrication, restoration, and hands-on build projects.

The product should help hobbyists and independent builders remember what they did, what comes next, which parts are needed or staged, how costs are trending, and what evidence exists in photos and logs.

## Product Lens

- Reliable project tracking
- Build-stage clarity
- Parts and cost visibility
- Photo and documentation continuity
- Mobile usability in a garage or shop
- Fast data entry
- Offline resilience
- Clear project history
- Affordable usefulness for hobbyists and independent builders
- Validation against Howard's Willys Wagon workflow when appropriate

## Positioning

Fabricator is not enterprise project management. It should feel like a focused mobile workspace for real builders working a few hours at a time.

## Pricing Direction

Pricing is proposed, not live, unless separately confirmed.

- Pro around $4.99-$7.99 monthly
