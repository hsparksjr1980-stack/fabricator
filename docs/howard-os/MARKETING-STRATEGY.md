# Marketing Strategy

Fabricator should be positioned as a premium but practical builder operating system for people managing vehicle, fabrication, restoration, and hands-on build projects.

## Audience

Primary audiences include hobbyist builders, classic vehicle owners, restorers, fabricators, off-road builders, garage project people, and independent hands-on builders.

## Message

Fabricator helps builders keep project context, parts, costs, photos, and next steps together so work can resume with less friction.

## Marketing Limits

Do not claim cloud sync is fully live, collaboration is live, production file uploads are live, every advisor provider is live, AI is fully live beyond verified capabilities, guaranteed time savings, guaranteed cost savings, guaranteed completion, professional engineering certification, safety certification, or that a placeholder or mock is production-ready.

## Weekly Limit

Complete no more than one approved marketing task per weekly cycle.

Nothing may be published, sent, or used for external contact without Howard approval.
