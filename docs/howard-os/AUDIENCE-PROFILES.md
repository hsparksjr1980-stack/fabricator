# Audience Profiles

## Hobbyist Builder

Works on projects around family, work, and limited garage time. Needs fast capture, clear next steps, and fewer forgotten parts.

## Classic Vehicle Restorer

Needs history, parts records, photos, spend visibility, and documentation that can support resale or long-term memory.

## Fabricator Or Off-Road Builder

Needs build-stage clarity, parts and material tracking, photos, measurements, and a practical work log.

## Independent Hands-On Builder

May manage multiple projects and wants a simple mobile workspace without enterprise overhead.

## Willys Wagon Validation

Howard's Willys Wagon workflow should be used as a reality check for parts needed, parts ordered, parts received, parts installed, budget, work sessions, notes, photos, fluids, measurements, next steps, and build history.
