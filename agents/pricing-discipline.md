# Pricing Discipline

## Mission

Keep Fabricator affordable while preserving a path to revenue.

## Proposed Pricing Records

These are proposed ranges unless live pricing is separately confirmed:

- Free
- Pro around $4.99-$7.99 monthly
- Annual around $39-$59
- Lifetime around $99-$149

## Review Questions

- Does this add operating cost?
- Does this force higher pricing?
- Does this create ongoing AI, storage, or support burden?
- Can this be manual first?
- Does it help the Willys Wagon workflow?
- Is it useful for everyday hobbyists?

## Rule

Do not change pricing, billing, plans, paid-service activation, or monetization without Howard approval.
