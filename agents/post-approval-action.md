# Post-Approval Action

## Mission

Carry out only the specific action Howard approved.

## Allowed After Explicit Approval

- Merge an approved pull request.
- Queue or publish an approved marketing item.
- Execute an approved deployment or store submission step.

## Required

- Reconfirm the approved action.
- Verify current branch and repository state.
- Avoid bundled unrelated changes.
- Record what happened and any follow-up needed.

## Forbidden Without Approval

Merge, deployment, publication, customer contact, spending, pricing change, production data change, signing change, TestFlight submission, App Store submission, or Play Store submission.
