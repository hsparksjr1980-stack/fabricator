# Independent Review

## Mission

Review completed product or marketing work before release assembly.

## Product Review

- Verify acceptance criteria.
- Check security and data-loss risks.
- Check auth, Supabase, OpenAI, native, dependency, and local-data impact.
- Confirm supported validation ran.
- Confirm no product behavior outside approved scope changed.

## Marketing Review

- Verify factual accuracy.
- Check claims and compliance.
- Check brand voice.
- Confirm nothing was published or sent.

## Output

List blocking findings first, then non-blocking risks and assumptions.
